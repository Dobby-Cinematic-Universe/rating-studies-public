\section{Participants, data and code}\label{sec:materials}

\subsection{Participants}\label{sec:participants}

Participants were recruited on CloudResearch Connect: adults who read English, with an approval rate of 95\% or
more, on a desktop computer or tablet. Each person took part once, in one study. The platform's demographic
data were not requested, so nothing else is known about the raters. The studies were not reviewed by an ethics
board.

\paragraph{What participants were told.} Before agreeing, each participant read what the task is (two practice
excerpts, eight excerpts of about 250 words, six or eight questions on each), what the texts are (machine-made
transcripts of publicly available online videos, informal, with errors and swearing), what is recorded (the
answers, the time spent on each page, and the participant code the platform passes on; no name and no contact
details), how they are paid, and that they can stop at any time. In the second study the listing and the
consent page also said that the excerpts may include heavy swearing, that about one excerpt in twelve includes
sexual language, that nobody reads more than one such excerpt, and that people who would rather not read this
kind of material should not take part.

\paragraph{Pay and time.} The platform requires at least \$7.50 an hour for the stated time. The first study
stated 18 minutes at \$2.25 and took a median of 21, so every participant of its main batch received \$0.65
more, and three whom the platform had timed out were paid in full afterwards. The second study stated 25
minutes at \$3.15 and took a median of 24 (Figure~\ref{fig:s2minutes}).

\paragraph{Checks, exclusions and rejections.} A session counted when the participant answered an
instructed-response item correctly and let at least 15 seconds pass before the first answer on five of the
eight excerpts. Participants who failed a check were paid like the others; their ratings do not count and
their set was given to someone else. There are two exceptions: in the second study two submissions were
rejected, following the platform's guidance, because several measures together showed that the excerpts had not
been read (the first answer within seconds of each excerpt appearing, about two thirds of the answers on one
level, no agreement with other raters). The listing had not named criteria for rejection in advance. Who is
valid was decided by the two checks alone.

\subsection{Data and code}\label{sec:data}

The package that holds this report also holds the data and one script.
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{data/study1\_ratings.csv} and \texttt{data/study2\_ratings.csv}: every rating of every valid
  rater, with the rater's form and set. Raters are numbered in a random order and sets carry random labels.
\item \texttt{data/pool.csv}: the three models' scores of every eligible candidate passage on the sixteen
  questions and the eight dimensions, with the site's recording scores, the recording's identifier in the public corpus and the excerpt's place in
  its transcript. \texttt{data/study1\_excerpts.csv} and \texttt{data/study2\_excerpts.csv} say which
  candidates were rated.
\item \texttt{data/study2\_model\_calls.csv}: the four calls of each model on each excerpt of the second study.
\item \texttt{replicate.py}: recomputes every figure of this report from these files with fixed random seeds,
  and with \texttt{--check} compares the result with the numbers reported. It needs Python with numpy and scipy.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What the data do not hold.} Nothing that identifies a participant: no participant code, no time of
day, no order of arrival, no closing comment and no time per answer. Time on the page is given as counts in
steps of five minutes and as summaries, not for single raters. No excerpt text: an excerpt is given by its
recording and its place in the transcript, so that it can be read in the public corpus by those who wish to. No
mark of which excerpts hold sensitive language. Three things in this report can therefore not be recomputed
from the public data: the first study under its reading check as first fixed, the second study without the
fourteen excerpts with sensitive language, and the earlier run of the second study.
